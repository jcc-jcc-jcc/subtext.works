Subtext Works is an advisory practice for creative companies and brands. Based in New York City, we work with founders, executives, boards and family offices across Europe and the US, providing strategic counsel, taking on defined projects and stepping into fractional leadership roles. Our work focuses on turning ambitious creative visions into companies built to last.

Subtext Works was founded by Johan Christian Chen. As CEO of Frama from 2016 to 2024, he led its growth into a global brand spanning furniture, lighting, body care and hospitality. Johan is also co-owner of the design publication Ark Journal.
